# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Disabled adults and people with temporary or chronic movement limitations who want to complete self-directed general-wellness workouts. They may be exercising from a phone, tablet, or laptop at home and need clear compatibility guidance, forgiving controls, and confidence that a workout respects their available movement, equipment, and comfort.

## Product Purpose

AdaptFit turns a saved movement profile into safe, understandable exercise discovery, compatible workout generation, guided sessions, non-clinical form feedback, and useful progress history. Success means a user can choose or generate a suitable workout, understand cautions and alternatives, exercise with or without camera tracking, review their results, and stay in control of their data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, encouraging, capable. The product should feel supportive without sounding clinical, patronizing, or aggressively athletic. Feedback celebrates consistency and control while respecting the user's autonomy.

## Anti-references

Avoid hospital and rehabilitation-software aesthetics, macho or punishment-oriented fitness language, generic pastel SaaS dashboards, dense analytics without plain-language meaning, and interfaces that assume every body moves the same way. Never present camera tracking as mandatory or compatibility guidance as a diagnosis.

## Design Principles

1. Adapt before excluding. Explain compatibility and offer useful alternatives.
2. Keep the user in control. Camera tracking, cautions, pauses, retries, and exits all need clear choices.
3. Make progress legible. Pair metrics with plain-language meaning and next steps.
4. Reward consistency, not perfection. Use warm, specific coaching without pressure or shame.
5. Preserve trust. Be explicit about privacy, non-clinical scope, data handling, and recoverable errors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard and switch-style navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, text zoom, high-contrast readable type, reduced motion, and information that does not rely on color alone. Interactive targets should be at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. Every camera-assisted flow must have an equally clear path without tracking, and time-based exercise controls must support pause, restart, and early completion.
